# Known limitations

- All operational records are saved in the current browser's IndexedDB and do not synchronize across employees/devices.
- Cash and Charge to Room are **simulations only**, not real collections, postings, Front Desk balances, receivables or gateway integrations.
- No employee accounts or authenticated actor attribution; details show `Not recorded`.
- Human-readable order references preserve complete UUID identity in base36; they are intentionally not globally sequenced.
- Ordering workflow is intended for frontend demonstration. Real payment handling needs authenticated, backend-authoritative transactions, server-generated references, idempotency, and reconciled Front Desk postings.
- Rooms Manager still includes its existing demonstration fallback room directory if Admin has not saved a configuration; no additional room data are generated by Orders.
- Large history scaling is limited by the existing snapshot-style IndexedDB writes and per-collection record limits; future production backend requires pagination and indexed queries.
- Actual Vite build, native typecheck, lint, formatting and live browser QA remain unverified until dependencies are installed.
